How the estimates work & important assumptions

Rates: The 6.5% resale baseline, +1 percentage-point assistance adjustment, and 4.5% / 5.5% new-home rates are user-provided examples, not verified current market rates or loan offers. The calculator assumes permanent fixed rates, not temporary buydowns. Enter the actual note rate directly. Selecting assistance does not automatically change that rate. “DPA” means down payment assistance; eligibility and combination with builder offers must be confirmed.

FHA example: Default down payment is 3.5%, subject to eligibility. Upfront mortgage insurance defaults to 1.75% of the base loan. Choose financing or payment at closing. Annual MIP defaults to 0.55% and is editable; select the applicable premium with your lender. Monthly MIP is an initial estimate based on the base loan, not an exact servicing schedule. Loan limits and qualification are not evaluated.

Other loans: Conventional defaults to 5% down and an editable 0.5% annual PMI estimate, omitted at 20% down. VA has no monthly mortgage insurance; select first use, subsequent use, or confirmed funding-fee exemption. Its fee adjusts to the down payment. USDA defaults to illustrative 1% upfront and 0.35% annual fees; confirm current program fees with the lender. Annual mortgage insurance / guarantee charges use the initial base balance as an approximation, not an exact amortizing fee schedule. Cash purchases omit all financing charges. Eligibility, loan limits, appraisals and program-specific seller-credit caps require lender review. Source: VA funding-fee schedule · CFPB mortgage insurance.

Listing imports: Public URL imports attempt structured listing data and clearly labeled text. They do not sign in, execute listing scripts, or bypass site restrictions. Some sites, including Zillow, may block automated access; use copied listing text when that happens. Community starting prices, multiple homes, and incomplete pages may need manual entry. Found values are not applied until reviewed. Saved comparison files include property details and your assumptions on your device; hosted URL requests are sent to the listing source.

Assistance: Enter only approved assistance applied to the down payment. It is capped at the required down payment here. Assistance does not reduce the mortgage a second time. Choose a grant or loan type; add a separate monthly payment for repayable assistance. Deferred or forgivable assistance may become due on sale, refinancing, or failure to satisfy program terms.

Closing costs: The $6,000–$13,000 range is a planning scenario, not a universal range. Include lender/title fees, prepaids and initial escrow deposits, and any borrower-paid points in the gross estimate; exclude down payment, financed FHA upfront MIP, separately entered agent compensation, inspections, and repairs. Seller/builder credits are capped at entered closing costs in this calculator and cannot offset the down payment. Actual loan-program limits may reduce allowable credits.

Commission: Buyer-agent compensation is negotiable, not a mandatory 3% builder commission. Enter only the portion the buyer actually owes after separately negotiated seller or builder payment. Do not count that payment again in closing credits.

Ownership budget: Property tax uses the full purchase price as a planning proxy for assessed value; enter the effective rate after any confirmed exemptions. New construction taxes must account for the completed house, not only vacant land. No appreciation, resale proceeds, tax benefits, or future repair claims are forecast.

Source references: HUD mortgage insurance schedule · NAR written buyer agreements. Confirm current terms against your Loan Estimate, purchase contract, assistance agreement, and warranty booklet.
